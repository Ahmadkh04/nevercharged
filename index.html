Everything you need to stop surprise charges

All features are free. Nothing to sign up for.

🛡️

Catches trials as you sign up

Reads “7-day free trial, then $12.99/month” on checkout pages and offers to track it. It always asks first.

⏰

Reminds you before the charge

7 days, 3 days, 1 day or 6 hours before, your choice. One click to the cancel page.

✉️

Finds trial emails

Started a trial on your phone? Open the confirmation in Gmail or Outlook and NeverCharged reads the exact end date.

✨

On-device AI

Messy pages are read by the AI built into Chrome and Edge, right on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

💤

“Still using it?”

Flags subscriptions you haven’t opened in weeks, and shows what they cost you each month.

📅

Add to calendar

Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar in one click, so reminders reach your phone too.

📈

Price-increase alerts

Warns you when a service shows a higher price than the one you pay.

💰

See what you save

Your monthly subscription total, plus a “Saved” counter for every charge you avoided.

🔄

Sync your computers

Optional. Uses your browser account’s own sync. There’s no NeverCharged server.

Private by design

NeverCharged has no servers. Your list stays in your browser, and only the features you turn on can read anything.

  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking
  • Pages and emails are read on your device, never uploaded
  • Reading pages, emails and history is off until you switch it on
  • Export or delete your data at any time
